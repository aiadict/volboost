Good to know

Three things to know.

250

The icon shows the level.

While a tab is boosted, its level appears on the toolbar icon, so you always know which tab is loud.

The limiter stops crackle.

Turning sound up 6× would normally clip and crackle. The limiter catches the peaks, so it just gets louder.

VoiceBassNight

Modes fix real problems.

Voice for mumbled dialogue and quiet calls. Bass for small laptop speakers. Night evens out loud scenes.

Private by design

Nothing leaves your computer.

All sound processing happens on your device. No account, no ads, no analytics, no browsing history. Volume Booster only touches a tab when you turn the knob.
