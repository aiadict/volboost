Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026 · applies from version 1.1.0

Volume Booster (“the Extension”) is a Chrome extension that makes tab audio louder or quieter on your device. This policy explains what data it collects, which is none.

Data we collect

We collect no personal data of any kind. The Extension does not:

  • collect, transmit or store your browsing history
  • record or transmit any audio
  • send any data to external servers
  • use analytics, tracking pixels or third-party SDKs
  • require an account

Permissions used

  • activeTab: to work on the tab you opened the Extension on.
  • tabCapture: to capture that tab’s audio on your device so it can be amplified.
  • offscreen: to process the audio in a background page using the Web Audio API.
  • scripting: to add a small fullscreen helper to the boosted tab only, so fullscreen keeps working during a boost.
  • windows: to put the browser window into fullscreen when a video requests it during a boost.
  • storage: to keep your settings and the small counters described below, on your device.

All audio processing happens on your device. No audio ever leaves your browser.

What is stored on your device

  • The current boost level and mode of the boosted tab. This is cleared when you close the browser.
  • Your Limiter setting (on or off).
  • The install date, how many times you started a boost, and the last few days you used it. These are used only to decide when to show a one-time request to rate the Extension.
  • Whether you already answered that rating request.

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you remove the Extension.

Pages the Extension opens

After installation, the Extension opens a welcome page that is part of the Extension itself and works offline. When you remove the Extension, Chrome opens a short feedback page on this website. No data is attached to that page’s address. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process visitors’ IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement. The website has no analytics or cookies of its own.

Third parties

The Extension does not integrate with any third-party services, advertising networks or analytics platforms. The share and rating buttons only open links you click. If you choose to send feedback through the form linked from the help and uninstall pages, Google Forms receives what you type there; nothing is filled in automatically.

Children’s privacy

The Extension does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new “Last updated” date.
