If something went wrong, there may be a quick fix. Pick what happened.

It didn’t work on a site

Reloading the tab and turning the knob again fixes most cases. Chrome’s own pages, like Settings and the Web Store, can’t be boosted by any extension. See Help for more.

The sound got distorted

Version 1.1 added a limiter that stops crackle at high levels. Make sure its orange light is on in the popup.

Fullscreen didn’t behave

Press Reset, enter fullscreen, then boost again. If a specific site still misbehaves, please tell us which one.
