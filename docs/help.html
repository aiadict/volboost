Help

Something not working?

Most problems have a quick fix. Open the topic that matches.

No sound, or the sound stopped, after boosting
  1. Reload the tab (⌘R on Mac, Ctrl+R on Windows).
  2. Start the video or music again.
  3. Open Volume Booster and turn the knob once more.

If it still stays silent, press Reset in the popup, then turn the knob again.

“Chrome doesn’t allow boosting on this page”

Chrome doesn’t let extensions capture sound on its own pages, such as Settings, New Tab, the Extensions page or the Chrome Web Store. Any normal website works.

The sound is distorted or crackles

Check that the Limiter key in the popup has its orange light on. The limiter stops crackle at high levels. If the source itself is already distorted, a lower level or the Voice mode usually sounds cleaner.

A boost stopped when I boosted another tab

Volume Booster boosts one tab at a time for now. Turning the knob on a new tab moves the boost there, and the popup tells you when that will happen.

Fullscreen

Fullscreen works while a tab is boosted: the whole window goes fullscreen. Press Esc to leave it as usual. If a site misbehaves, press Reset, enter fullscreen, then boost again.

What do Voice, Bass and Night do?
  • Voice brings speech forward: films with mumbled dialogue, quiet people on calls, lectures.
  • Bass adds low end for small laptop speakers.
  • Night makes quiet parts louder and loud parts softer, so you don’t have to keep changing the volume.
What does the number on the icon mean?

It’s the boost level of that tab: 250 means 250%. No number means the tab plays at its normal level.

Turning it back off

Press Reset, or turn the knob back to 100 with the Normal mode selected. The tab goes back to its normal sound right away.

Still stuck?

Send us a short note with the site and what happened. It takes a minute. You can also open an issue on GitHub.
